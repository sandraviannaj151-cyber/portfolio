1. Text Generation & Hallucination Detection (RLHF) Sample 01
Scenario / Prompt:

A user asks for a summary of recent tax regulations in Brazil. Model A fabricates a non-existent law number while maintaining a confident tone, whereas Model B accurately cites the real legislation with minor formatting flaws.

Ranked Model B significantly higher over Model A.

Evaluating a synthesized Portuguese voice output reading a customer service script containing technical acronyms and natural human pauses.

Evaluation Decision:

Flagged prosody unnaturalness on acronym pronunciation and logged exact timestamp discrepancies in verbatim transcription.

Rubric Rationale:

Accurate phonetic rendering of domain-specific terms and strict adherence to hesitation/breath markers are essential for training reliable ASR and natural-sounding Text-to-Speech (TTS) models.

Assessing whether an AI assistant's map recommendation and visual description match an uploaded storefront photo and user location intent.

Marked as Partially Accurate — Visual Mismatch.
